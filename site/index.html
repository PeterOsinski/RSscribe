Features

Everything on your machine.

Your voice never leaves your Mac. Whisper runs on your GPU, the transcript goes straight to your cursor. There's also a CLI for one-off recordings and file transcription (WAV, MP3, FLAC, OGG, OPUS, M4A).

🔒

Fully local

Transcription runs through whisper.cpp on Apple Silicon (Metal). No network, no account, no data collection.

🌍

~99 languages

Auto-detects your language. English and Polish are tested daily; everything Whisper knows works out of the box.

⦿

Menu-bar status

A dot in your macOS menu bar shows the state at a glance — grey when idle, red while listening.

⌥

Double-tap Option

Double-tap Option to start listening, double-tap again to stop. No keystroke leaks into your app.

⚡

Real-time paste

Dictate and the words appear as you speak — each phrase is pasted the moment you pause.

🎚️

Pick your model

From tiny to large-v3-turbo, quantized or full, to trade speed against accuracy.

How it works

Three steps to dictation.

01

Double-tap Option

The dot turns red and the microphone goes live. That's the whole gesture.

02

Start talking

A voice-activity detector captures each phrase as you pause for breath.

03

It types

Each phrase appears as you speak — transcribed locally and pasted into whatever you're looking at.

Install

Up and running in a minute.

Requires macOS 14+ on Apple Silicon and Homebrew. The Whisper model downloads automatically on first use.

# 1. Install Rust
curl --proto '=https' --tlsv1.2 -sSf https://sh.rustup.rs | sh

# 2. Install whisper.cpp and ffmpeg
brew install whisper.cpp ffmpeg

# 3. Build
cargo build --release

# 4. Run the daemon
./target/release/voice-paste
